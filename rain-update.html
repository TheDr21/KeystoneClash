NEW BRACKET VENUE

No Offseason · Tarentum

306 Chase Drive, Tarentum, PA 15084 — Open in Maps.

Original complex (pool play)
East End Park, 51 Meadow St, McDonald, PA 15057. Two fields. Overflow parking on East O’Hara St.
Rules
USA Softball rules. 90 minutes, finish the inning. Two umpires every game. Courtesy runner for pitcher and catcher. Run rule 12 after 3, 10 after 4, 8 after 5. Game official after three full innings; rain mid-inning reverts to last completed inning. Full Rules
Format (updated)
Pool play Friday evening and Saturday morning — complete. Bracket was scheduled Saturday afternoon at East End; rescheduled due to rain. Remaining bracket is single elimination at No Offseason. First-round losers play consolation; winners continue.
