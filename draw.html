How this draw works

You should not have to take our word for who won. Two published facts make the result checkable by anyone.

  1. The entry list is frozen before the draw. When sales close we publish raffle-entries.json — ticket numbers only, no names or emails — and a SHA-256 fingerprint of it. Change one ticket afterward and the fingerprint no longer matches.
  2. The randomness comes from outside. The winning number is derived from a drand beacon round chosen before that round exists. drand is run by an independent group of organizations, published on a fixed schedule, and identical for everyone. We cannot pick it, predict it, or retry it.
  3. The winner is arithmetic. Winning ticket = SHA256(commitment + randomness) mod total + 1. Same inputs, same answer, every time, on anyone's computer.

Verify it yourself

Clone the repository and run the checker. It re-fetches the beacon from drand directly and recomputes the result.

git clone https://github.com/TheDr21/KeystoneClash
cd KeystoneClash
python3 scripts/verify_draw.py

Find your own tickets: your Zeffy receipt shows a payment id. The ref in the entry list is the first 12 characters of its SHA-256, so you can locate your ticket range without your name ever appearing on this page.
